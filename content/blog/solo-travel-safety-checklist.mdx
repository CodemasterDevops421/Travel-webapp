## Start with arrival logistics

Know your route from airport or station to hostel before landing. Keep one offline map and verify check-in policy for late arrivals.

## Protect your documents

Store encrypted copies of passport, visa, and insurance in a secure cloud location. Share your high-level movement plan with one emergency contact.

## Choose context over hype

Recent reviews about staff responsiveness and neighborhood safety are usually better signals than top-line rating alone.

## Daily safety routine

- Use hostel lockers for valuables
- Avoid displaying expensive electronics in transit hubs
- Keep transport backup options after midnight
