## Why booking timing matters

For most high-demand destinations, the best value usually appears around three to six weeks before check-in. At this stage, inventory is still healthy and rates are less likely to reflect last-minute scarcity.

## Peak season changes the rule

Holiday calendars and festivals shift pricing behavior. Budget rooms sell out first, and refundable inventory gets more expensive as demand spikes.

### What to check before paying

- Cancellation window and fee terms
- Deposit policy and refund timeline
- Final price after taxes and city fees

## Flexible search wins

Instead of fixating on one property, keep two or three neighborhoods in scope. This improves your chances of finding a better total value with stronger policy terms.
