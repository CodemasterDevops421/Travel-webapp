## Start with your trip anchor

Pick the primary experience first, such as nightlife, coworking, or museum access. Then optimize location around that anchor.

## Measure transfer complexity

A direct 35-minute route usually beats a 20-minute route with two transfers and poor late-night availability.

## Check night-time livability

Confirm late food options, convenience stores, and safe return transport from your likely evening zones.

## Cost is total, not nightly

A higher nightly rate in the right area often reduces total trip cost by saving time and daily transit spend.
