## Day 1: settle and reset

Pick one beach stretch, one cafe, and one sunset viewpoint. Avoid over-scheduling your first day.

## Day 2: choose north or south

Trying to cover both sides in one day creates unnecessary transit friction. Go deeper in one zone instead.

### Suggested pace

- Morning swim and breakfast
- Midday market walk
- Golden-hour coastal stop

## Day 3: low-intensity close

Keep the final day simple with brunch and a relaxed transfer plan. This keeps the return leg far less stressful.
