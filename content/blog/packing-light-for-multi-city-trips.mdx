## Build around one base palette

Choose a neutral color base and layer around it to reduce duplicate items and improve outfit combinations.

## Use a two-cube system

One compression cube for clothing and one utility pouch for cables, medicine, and documents keeps transitions fast.

## Plan laundry rhythm

For multi-city plans, schedule light laundry intervals instead of carrying extra volume from day one.

## Arrival-first access

Keep your day-one essentials in your top compartment so late check-ins stay frictionless.
