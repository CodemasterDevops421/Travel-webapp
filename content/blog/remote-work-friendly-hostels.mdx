## Validate Wi-Fi behavior, not promises

Check recent reviews for evening upload stability because daytime speed tests are often misleading.

## Workspace matters

Look for quiet zones and clear policies for meetings. Shared dorm spaces are rarely suitable for calls.

## Power and backup

Confirm power backup quality, charging access, and desk ergonomics if you depend on daily video sessions.

## Long-stay leverage

If staying more than a week, request weekly pricing and workspace inclusions before checkout.
