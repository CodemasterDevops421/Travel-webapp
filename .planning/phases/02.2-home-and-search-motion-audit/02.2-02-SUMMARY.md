## Decisions Made

- Used CSS-based motion primitives instead of introducing a new client hero component so the homepage stays server-first and the search shell remains structurally stable.
- Kept the rotating value language decorative with a static `motion-reduce` fallback to satisfy the accessibility contract without removing the stronger brand layer.
- Standardized homepage section motion through one reusable reveal marker instead of separate section animations to reduce drift and keep mobile safety testable.
