## Validation Goals

This plan establishes the baseline guardrails for all later homepage and search-shell motion work:

- keep the homepage hero search-first and high-contrast
- keep compact desktop search inline on non-home routes
- keep checkout routes free of shared header chrome
- prohibit scroll-jacking and wheel/touch interception in discovery-shell code
- require reduced-motion-safe motion decisions before decorative animation ships
